WarBreeds Controls

Every keyboard and mouse control in a mission, as the original game handles them, followed by what the WarBreeds HD patch adds. The list comes from reading the game's code (the manual's own list is incomplete), so a few rarely used keys are marked likely where the exact effect still needs confirming in play.

Mouse

The original scheme uses the left button for everything: it selects, and with your units selected it gives orders. The right button clears the selection. The cursor shape always shows what a left click will do: an animated arrow for move, red for attack or target.

ActionResult
Left-click your unitSelect it (replaces the selection). Never gives an order.
Shift+left-click your unitAdd it to the selection, or remove it if it's already selected.
Left-click your buildingSelect the building, even with units selected. Units clicked onto a building they can enter enter it instead.
Left-click an enemyWith your units selected: attack it. With nothing selected: select it to see its details.
Left-click a resource or podWith units selected: target it (the units decide what to do, such as harvest).
Left-click the groundWith your units selected: move there. Fogged ground works too.
Left-drag a boxSelect your units inside it (replaces the selection). A drag shorter than about 32 pixels counts as a click.
Double-click your buildingSwitch it on or off (it must be finished and selected).
Double-click an alert iconThe icons down the left edge: centre the camera on that event.
Right-clickClear the selection. This also cancels a Shaman's build, plant, repair or upgrade mode.
Right-click the minimapMove the selected units to that point.
Left-click or drag the minimapMove the camera there (it follows while you hold the button).
Screen edgesScroll the camera: 35 pixels deep at the top and bottom, 30 at the sides. Hold Shift to scroll twice as fast.

The original game ignores a quick second right-click (Windows reports it as a double-click), and it doesn't use the middle button or the mouse wheel.

These act on a selection of several units.

Shamans (builders)

These keys work with exactly one of your Shamans selected. They open the same pages as the info-panel buttons.

KeyAction
BBuild: opens the list of buildings. Pick one, then click where it goes.
PPlant: opens the list of pods. Pick one, then click pod soil (the pale lavender ground).
RRepair: click one of your buildings. This is also the only way to finish a building whose builder was killed.
UUpgrade: click one of your buildings.
GGet Genes (only once you have genes to collect).

Leaving a mode

Press the panel's cancel button, right-click (this also clears the selection), or select something else. Esc opens the game menu instead.

Placement rules

Buildings need power in range; a Refinery needs a pod planted nearby. A refused spot just ignores the click. Repair also refuses buildings that are unpowered or switched off.

WarBreeds HD

Everything above still works the same unless noted. Most additions can be switched off on the main menu's Settings page.

Builders and fog

ControlAction
Click a Shaman onto your unfinished or damaged buildingIt goes and finishes or repairs it (the game's Repair order). The building must be powered and switched on.
Plant mode, click fogged groundThe Shaman walks there and plants. Buildings could always be placed in fog. In build or plant mode the cursor over fog shows that the Shaman will walk.
Several Shamans selected (and nothing else)The panel shows the build, plant, upgrade and repair buttons, and B, P, R, U and quick-build work. The original offered them only with exactly one Shaman. Settings → Controls → Group Build.
Place a building with several ShamansThe nearest Shaman starts it. The others walk to its edge and help build it as soon as it stands. If the building can't go there, nothing happens and you stay in build mode, as with one Shaman.
Plant, repair or cancel with several ShamansThe nearest Shaman plants the pod. Repair sends them all. The panel's cancel stops them all. Upgrade and Get Genes act on the first Shaman only.

Modern mouse scheme

Settings → Controls → Mouse Scheme. Classic (the original, described at the top) is the default.

ControlAction
Left-clickSelect only. Empty ground clears the selection; Shift adds or removes your units; box selection as before.
Right-clickGive the order the cursor shows: move, attack, harvest, enter. Builders onto your unfinished or damaged building repair it.
Right-click in a modeCancel build, plant, repair or upgrade, keeping the Shaman selected.
Right-click the minimapMove there, as before.
Hold a key and left-clickUnchanged: A, Tab, Space, G and the others still use the left button.

In both schemes a quick second right-click now counts, where the original dropped it.

Map editor

Main menu → Map Editor. The panel sits in the top-right corner, with tabs: Terrain, Things, Units, Players, Map, Events and AI. Painting works like the original editor's smart palette: terrain blends into its neighbours, and a terrain only goes next to the terrains it has transitions for (Lava next to Tar, for example). When a stroke or a placement can't go somewhere, the panel says why. An outline at the cursor shows the cells a brush will paint, the rectangle a fill will cover, or the ground a thing will take.

ControlAction
Tab, or Hide / Show (Tab)Fold the panel to a one-line strip and open it again. The chosen terrain, brush or thing stays in use, so you can paint and place where the panel was.
Terrain: left-dragPaint the chosen terrain with the chosen brush (1, 2, 3 or 5 cells).
Terrain: Shift+left-dragFill a rectangle.
Terrain: right-clickPick the terrain under the cursor.
Things: left-click or dragPlace trees, rocks, ruins or crops (one per tile). Trees and rocks go on their own subtype (crushed crystal, roots, hardened tar), ruins on open ground, crops on cropland.
Things: right-clickRemove the crop, tree, rock or ruin under the cursor.
Things: Fill Pods / Spores / Weeds, Clear CropsEvery empty cropland tile gets a crop; or every crop goes.
Units: Creatures / Devices, then left-clickPlace one for the chosen player (< > on the panel). Devices are placed complete, with or without power.
Units: Select, then left-click / dragSelect a thing; drag it to move it. The panel then sets hit points (- +; right-click for single points), rank and gizmos (click for the next, right-click for the previous), or deletes it (Del works too). Right-click deselects.
Players: the panelType (Computer, Human, Either, None; player 1 is always Human), race, CGK level per clan (click +1, right-click -1) and available clans.
Players: left-click on the mapPut the chosen player's start point there (a numbered ring in its colour).
Map: the panelName (click, type, Enter), fog (fog and shroud, fog only, none), players supported and recommended. The size is shown only.
Events tabOpens the event (trigger) editor over the map, the game's own screen for it. Its buttons, or the keys below, step through and edit the map's events.
Events: Shift / Ctrl / Ctrl+Shift with ← → Home EndPrevious / next / first / last event, condition or action.
Events: the same modifier with A / RAdd / remove an event, condition or action.
Events: *, EscMake the event persistent (it can fire again); close the event editor.
AI tabEach computer player's squad, base, scouting and influence settings (1 to 5). A skirmish on a map you made uses them (the original game always replaced them with the clan defaults; the game's own maps still do, so they play as they always have). An AI AGGRESSION event changes them during play.
CheckList what may be wrong with the map: missing start points or Shamans, devices without power, start points cut off from each other, cropland leaving a narrow gap at the edge and more. Saving runs the same check and only warns.
Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+YUndo / redo: strokes, placements, moves, deletions and settings.
Arrow keys, mouse wheel, minimapScroll and zoom, as in a mission.
Save As, then type a nameUp to 8 letters or digits; Enter saves, Esc cancels. Built-in maps are never overwritten; an existing map of yours asks first.
Test Map, twiceSave, then start a skirmish on the map. The first click only asks; anything else you do in between cancels it.
ExitBack to the map list. With unsaved changes, click it twice.
